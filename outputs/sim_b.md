# Sim B — basket excess returns (protocol: simulation/protocol.md)

Entry: first trading day on/after 1 July of T+1 (10-K season over); exit after 1/2/3 years; equal weight, price returns, excess over SPY. Baskets need ≥ 3 tickers with prices; tickers below $1 at entry are excluded and a ticker's return is capped at +500% (OTC-shell artifacts). Adjusted closes (splits, dividends). Prices end 2026-11-06. Ticker coverage at entry (tickers with prices ÷ tickers named in 10-Ks): 0.76 — missing tickers are mostly delisted firms (survivorship bias, same direction for every arm).

Columns: mean excess | cluster SE (by T) | t | median / hit rate | n events (T clusters)
